# Catalog image attribution

Product photos in this folder are freely licensed images from Wikimedia Commons,
downloaded locally so the app has no runtime image dependency. They replace the
placeholder-image service originally specified in SPEC.md §7.

- `iphone-17.jpg`, `iphone-17-alt.jpg` — "Black iPhone 17", "Mist Blue iPhone 17"
  by Ahmad Ali Karim, CC0.
- `iphone-17-pro-max.jpg`, `iphone-17-pro-max-alt.jpg` — "Silver iPhone 17 Pro Max",
  "Deep Blue iPhone 17 Pro Max" by Ahmad Ali Karim, CC0.
- `galaxy-s25-ultra.jpg`, `galaxy-s25-ultra-alt.jpg` — "Rear view of the Samsung
  Galaxy S25 Ultra smartphone", "Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra smartphone – side view"
  by HuangWending18072009, CC0.
- `pixel-10.jpg`, `pixel-10-alt.jpg` — "Avant / Arrière du Google Pixel 10 Pro
  pierre de lune de 256 Go" (front / back) by Fabe56, CC BY-SA 4.0. The photos
  show the Pixel 10 Pro in Moonstone; the base Pixel 10 is visually near-identical
  and this is a mock catalog.
- `macbook-air-m4.jpg`, `macbook-air-m4-alt.jpg` — "MacBook Air (13-inch, M4,
  Silver)" and "(15-inch, M4, Silver)" by AzureSaturn, CC0. The 15-inch photo
  stands in for the second 13-inch variant view; the design is near-identical.
- `oneplus-15.png`, `oneplus-15-alt.jpg` — "Oneplus-15-back.png" by Antoni Rajnoga,
  CC BY 4.0; "OnePlus 15 back.jpg" by Maksdroider, CC BY-SA 4.0.
